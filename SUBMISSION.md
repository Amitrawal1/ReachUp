## 2. Solution Statement (paste into the form)

> Replace `LIVE LINK` and `GITHUB LINK` before pasting.

```
ReachUp – A Bridge Between Rural Talent and Opportunities

Problem: Students in villages and small towns miss internships, jobs and scholarships because information is scattered, mostly in English, full of fake offers, and nobody tells them if they are eligible or how to apply.

Our prototype (Phase 1) is a mobile-first web app (installable PWA, works offline) in Hindi and English:

1. Sign up with mobile number and choose language.
2. Simple profile: 12 one-tap questions (education, stream, marks, state, category, family income, interests), with voice input.
3. Matched feed: opportunities ranked by eligibility, interests, stipend/benefit and deadline. Every card clearly says "You are eligible" or exactly what is missing.
4. Scam check: every listing is checked for 5 warning signs (asks for fee, unknown source, only personal WhatsApp/phone, too-good pay, pressure words). Fake listings are blocked and the student sees why.
5. Opportunity page: explained in simple words, "why it suits you", rule-by-rule eligibility with tips to become eligible, document checklist, step-by-step apply guide, read aloud, share on WhatsApp.
6. Deadline reminders: save an opportunity and get a notification 3 days before the last date.
7. My List: track each application (Saved → Applied → Interview → Selected / Not selected). If not selected, we suggest similar opportunities they are eligible for.
8. WhatsApp preview: how students without the app will get their weekly top matches (Phase 2).

Data: real government schemes (PM Internship Scheme, National Scholarship Portal, AICTE Pragati, PMKVY, NATS, NAPS, NCS, SIH and more) plus demo partner companies.

Tech: React + Vite + Tailwind, PWA with offline support, rule-based matching and scam engine, Web Speech API for voice and read-aloud. Next: WhatsApp Business API bot, live data from portals, ML matching, more Indian languages via Bhashini, organisation portal.

Live demo: LIVE LINK (tap "Try with a sample student" to skip sign-up)
Code: GITHUB LINK
```
