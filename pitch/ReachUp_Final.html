1. The Problem

Many talented students and young professionals live in small towns and villages. They have the skills and the interest, but they often do not know about the internships, jobs, scholarships, fellowships, training programs and competitions that are open to them. Students in big cities hear about these through seniors, college placement cells, LinkedIn and professional networks. Rural students usually do not have this network.

The main problems are:

  • Scattered information: opportunities are spread across many websites, apps, government portals and social media pages. There is no single place to see what is relevant.
  • Language barrier: most listings and application forms are only in English.
  • No network or guidance: students have no mentors or seniors to tell them which opportunity is right for them, how to apply, or how to prepare.
  • Low awareness of eligibility: many students think an opportunity is "not for them" and never apply, even when they are eligible.
  • Missed deadlines: by the time news reaches a small town, the last date has often passed.
  • Weak internet and basic phones: heavy websites do not work well on slow data or low-end phones.
  • Fake listings and scams: fake job offers that ask for registration fees target students who cannot easily verify them.
  • Hard application process: students struggle with resumes, cover letters, document uploads and interviews.

As a result, a lot of real talent is never discovered, and organisations miss out on capable people from outside big cities.

2. Our Solution

ReachUp is a mobile-first, multilingual platform that brings the right opportunities to rural and semi-urban youth and helps them all the way from finding an opportunity to getting selected.

It works on a simple idea: instead of asking students to search everywhere, we bring verified and relevant opportunities to them, in their own language, on the apps they already use, and we guide them until they apply.

The prototype runs fully in the browser: the student's profile and saved list stay on their own phone, which keeps data private and lets the app work offline. In the full product the same matching and scam-check logic will read from a live database filled by partner APIs, portal feeds and the organisation portal.

About the demo data: the government schemes in the prototype are real (PM Internship Scheme, National Scholarship Portal, Central Sector Scholarship, AICTE Pragati, PMKVY / Skill India, NATS, NAPS, AICTE internships, National Career Service, Smart India Hackathon), with simplified amounts and rules. Deadlines and the companies marked "demo partner" are sample data. Two fake listings are included on purpose to demonstrate the scam check.

10. Why This Solution Should Exist

  • Talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. A student's location should not decide whether they hear about a scholarship or an internship.
  • Many good opportunities go unused. Scholarships, government internships and training programs often get fewer applications from rural areas, not because students are not capable, but because they never hear about them.
  • Remote work has removed distance. Many internships and jobs can now be done from anywhere, but rural students still lack the link to find them.
  • Phones and WhatsApp are now common in villages. The channel to reach rural youth already exists; it only needs the right content in the right language.
  • Organisations want diverse talent. Companies and programs want to reach beyond big cities but have no easy way to do it.
  • Guidance changes outcomes. Just showing a listing is not enough; support with eligibility, resume and preparation is what turns awareness into selection.

14. How We Will Measure Impact

  • Number of students registered from rural and semi-urban areas.
  • Number of applications submitted through the platform.
  • Number of students selected for internships, jobs, scholarships or programs.
  • Time taken from sign-up to first application.
  • Number of organisations posting opportunities and hiring from the platform.
  • Number of fake listings caught before reaching students.

16. Conclusion

ReachUp connects rural and semi-urban talent to the opportunities they deserve. It brings verified, relevant opportunities to students in their own language and on the apps they already use, and it supports them at every step from discovery to selection. It also helps organisations find capable people beyond big cities.

Our working prototype already shows the core of this: a student answers a few simple questions in Hindi, sees which opportunities they are eligible for, is protected from fake offers, and is guided and reminded until they apply.

Our goal is simple: no talented student should miss an opportunity just because of where they live.
